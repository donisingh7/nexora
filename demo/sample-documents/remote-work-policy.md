# Remote Work Policy

This policy applies to all employees who work outside of a company office on a regular basis.

## Core hours

Remote employees are expected to be reachable between 10:00 and 15:00 in their local time zone, Monday through Friday. Outside of core hours, employees may arrange their schedule as they see fit, provided deadlines and meetings are met.

## Team sync

Each team holds a weekly video sync. Attendance is expected unless the employee has given advance notice to their manager. Cameras are optional.

## Equipment

The company provides a laptop, a monitor, and a headset for remote work. Requests for additional equipment go through the IT request form and are reviewed within five business days.

## Home office stipend

Employees who work remotely more than three days per week may claim a one-time home office stipend of up to $500 for desk, chair, or lighting purchases, submitted as a standard expense report.

## Security expectations

Remote employees must connect to internal systems only over the company VPN and must not use public, unsecured Wi-Fi networks to access confidential systems.
