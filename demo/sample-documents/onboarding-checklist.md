# New Hire Onboarding Checklist

This checklist covers the first two weeks for a new employee.

## Before day one

IT provisions a laptop, email account, and access badge. The hiring manager schedules a welcome meeting and assigns an onboarding buddy from the new hire's team.

## First week

New hires complete orientation, which covers company policies, benefits enrollment, and payroll setup. Equipment setup and required security training, including the data security guidelines, are completed by the end of the first week.

## Benefits enrollment

Health, dental, and retirement plan enrollment must be completed within thirty days of the start date. Enrollment forms are available through the HR portal, and the benefits team holds office hours every Wednesday for questions.

## Second week

By the end of the second week, new hires should have met with their manager to set initial thirty-, sixty-, and ninety-day goals, and completed any team-specific tooling setup.

## Ongoing support

The onboarding buddy remains available as a point of contact for informal questions through the end of the new hire's first ninety days.
