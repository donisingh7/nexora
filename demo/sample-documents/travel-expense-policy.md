# Travel and Expense Policy

This policy describes how employees should book travel and submit expenses for reimbursement.

## Booking travel

Flights and hotels for business trips should be booked through the approved travel portal at least seven days before departure when possible. Economy class is standard for flights under six hours; a manager's approval is required for premium economy or business class.

## Submitting expense reports

Expense reports must be submitted within thirty days of the trip's end date. Each line item requires an itemized receipt; credit card statements alone are not sufficient documentation. Reports submitted after the thirty-day window require written justification from the employee's manager.

## Meal allowances

Employees traveling on company business may claim up to $60 per day for meals without itemized receipts, provided the total does not exceed the daily cap. Alcohol is not reimbursable.

## Non-reimbursable expenses

The company does not reimburse personal entertainment, traffic or parking fines, or upgrades to travel class beyond what is approved in advance.

## Approval workflow

Expense reports under $500 are auto-approved after manager sign-off. Reports at or above $500 require an additional finance review, which typically takes three to five business days.
